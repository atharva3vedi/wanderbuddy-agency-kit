# Plans

## Where we are, November 2026

- **The app** is live on iPhone and Android.
- **The CRM** is live. Agencies run their business on it, each on its own branded address.
- **The marketplace** is live. Agencies list trips at wanderbuddyapp.com/trips, and travellers ask, hold a spot or book.

## What we are doing now

1. **Bring agencies on.** The pitch is bookings, not software: we list their trips, run ads for them from our own page, and give them the assistant, the CRM and the trip app.
2. **Bring travellers to those trips.** Trip search, trip pages people can find on Google, and ads.
3. **Grow the app and the CRM together.** The marketplace only works when both sides are already here.

Southeast Asia comes first: Thailand, Vietnam, Bali, Laos. Trips in India and Korea are listed too.

## Where marketing helps most

- Traveller ads that fill trips already listed. The first round is in [`ads/india-2026-11`](../ads/india-2026-11).
- Content that gets agencies to list. See [`ads/agency-reels`](../ads/agency-reels) and [`ads/b2b-cinematic`](../ads/b2b-cinematic).
- A steady organic feed of reels, carousels and memes. See [`reels`](../reels) and [`social`](../social).

## Next

- Online checkout for every listed trip, in more countries.
- More to book next to agency trips: activities, transport and hotels.
- In the app: shared trip photos and a live map for road trips.
