# Reel 1 (Walk-in): what to film

You and one friend, about 20 minutes. The friend plays the agency owner.

## Set up once

- Phone vertical, 1080p or 4K, 30 fps. Wipe the lens.
- A desk with a laptop. Light from a window in front of your faces, not behind.
- Nothing readable in frame: no shop sign, logo, ₹ price, local phone number, or a client's itinerary.
  Plain t-shirts with no print.
- Quiet room, fan and AC off. Phone within about 1 metre of whoever is talking.
- For the two shots with both of you in frame, prop the phone on a shelf or a stack of books.
- A stack of 8 to 10 plain printed pages on the desk (any text, it stays blurry).

## The takes

Do each one two or three times. Leave 2 seconds of silence before and after every line.

| # | Shot | Who films | Lines |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You push the door open and walk up to the desk, phone held at chest height | You | (no talking) |
| 2 | Both of you: owner at the laptop, you walk in and lean on the desk | Propped phone | You: "You sell trips to Bali?" Owner: "Bali, Vietnam, Thailand. Why?" |
| 3 | Close on you, chest up | Friend | "Send me your trips. I'll list every one on WanderBuddy and keep them live." |
| 4 | Close on the owner, one eyebrow up | You | "How much?" |
| 5 | Close on you, straight face, keep still for 2 seconds after | Friend | "Free." |
| 6 | Same as 3 | Friend | "Travellers find it there." Then: "And you run the whole trip from our CRM." |
| 7 | Both of you, propped phone | Propped phone | Owner: "So what do you need from me?" You: "Your trips. That's it." |
| 8 | Owner slides the stack over. You pick it up, walk to the door, turn back to the phone | Friend | You: "Next one." |

Say it "Wander Buddy", clearly, two words.
