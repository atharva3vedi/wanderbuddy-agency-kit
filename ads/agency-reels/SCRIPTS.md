## What the reels claim, and what they don't

- **"We list every trip and keep them live."** A promise of service. Until the listing desk (B1.7)
  ships, that means we list them by hand.
- **"Free."** Matches the plan's hook. B0.1 still has "listing stays free" to confirm, so posting
  these locks that in publicly.
- **"Travellers find it there."** The Community tab is live. Agency cards with price and seats
  (B1.4) are not in the app yet, so the trip card is a graphic, never dressed up as an app screenshot.
- **"Every enquiry, quote and booking in one CRM."** Live: the inbox, itineraries and bookings
  (bookings went live 10-28). The line never says the CRM is free.
- **Left out on purpose:** paying for a trip on WanderBuddy (Phase 2 is not live, so no reel says
  "book on WanderBuddy"), any fee, traveller numbers, dates for anything coming, the map.
