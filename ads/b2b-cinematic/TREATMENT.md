# Four films for agencies (9:16, about 25 s each)

One film per thing we sell an agency. Each one is built around a single picture
from art or design history, staged in Blender and lit like cinema, then lands on
the real screen that does the job, with the one azure ring. No floating-device
demo shots, no stock, nothing generated: every screen is a real capture of the
Lantern Trips sample agency.

## Rules kept (Brand Codex Rev 03)

- Real screens only, one azure ring, one Instrument Serif italic word per film, WbSans everything else.
- No yellow or amber light. Night is ink and cool white, dawn is pale azure and teal.
- Sound: the Pixabay bossa bed and the Iowa Steinway notes in G only, -14 LUFS. No synthesized whooshes or risers.
- Copy from the codex's own "We say" lines for agencies. No prices for our product, no fee, no "book on WanderBuddy", no em dashes, no exclamation marks.
- Lantern Trips and Kite Trips are the only agency names on screen.
- Text sits between 220 px from the top and 420 px from the bottom, clear of the Reels and TikTok buttons.
