# Music and sound shortlist for the second-pass films (2026-11-11)

Nothing here has been downloaded. The founder picks by ear; each download then needs a yes
(file, source, size). Every track is on Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License (commercial
use, no credit), the same licence as the bossa bed we already use.

How this was checked: about 60 track pages were opened and read by a text summariser, not by
ear or eye. "Clean" means the page showed neither an "AI generated" label nor a "Content ID
Registered" notice. Re-open the page before locking any track.

Main finding: most Pixabay tracks that fit were either AI-generated or Content ID registered,
which the Brand Codex bans. No clean hand-percussion track and no clean track with an East or
South-East Asian instrument was found, so films 2 and 5 are the weak spots.
