## Roles (split by format)
- **Video track -> wanderbuddy-f8**: POV skits, the "Group trip red flags" series, demos,
  campaign films, trend-jacks, itinerary/voice-room demos.
- **Static/text track -> WanderBuddy experiment planning**: carousels, blog/SEO, Reddit
  stories, Pinterest pins, static memes, UGC/community prompts.

## Rules
- Cadence: 6 posts/week, Mon-Sat. Sundays = repurpose the week's best into a 2nd platform.
- Brand copy: NO em dashes (use colons, commas, parens). En-dashes in ranges + arrows OK.
- Exact palette hex source of truth = `lib/theme/app_colors.dart`.
- House look: navy+amber (reddit-posters Edge system) for product/pain static; photo-led
  (Pexels) look reserved for destination/value carousels.
- Craft (video): hook/loop/payoff/bait, first frame IS the thumbnail, cut 30-50%.
- $0 first: founder-on-camera + demo harness (`lib/main_demo.dart` + `lib/demo/`, zero
  backend, pixel-matches UI). Higgsfield/Seedance only on the hybrid cells.

## ICP + wedge
ICP = the Organizer ("nobody has to be the spreadsheet person"). Wedge = language / the
live voice room. Pillar mix (deliberate): Pain ~50%, Value ~20%, Wedge ~15%, Trust ~15%.
Pain is heaviest for cold-start reach; wedge never drops below every few days (it's the moat).
