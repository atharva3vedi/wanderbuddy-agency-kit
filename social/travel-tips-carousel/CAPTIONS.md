## Accuracy notes

Every tip is a real rule, but two are worth a sanity check before you reply to comments:

- **Slide 6 (delay compensation).** This is EU261 and its UK equivalent. It covers any flight
  departing the EU or UK, and flights arriving there operated by an EU or UK airline.
  Compensation is banded by distance and tops out at 600 euros. It does not apply when the
  cause is genuinely extraordinary, for example weather or air traffic control strikes.
  The card says "can be worth up to", which is the honest version.
- **Slide 10 (112).** 112 is the single European emergency number and reaches services in
  every EU country, plus a lot of others. Whether a phone with no SIM connects at all depends
  on the local network operator, so the card says "almost anywhere" rather than "everywhere".

If someone in the comments pushes on either of these, agree with the nuance. Do not defend
an absolute we did not claim.
