# The People You Meet On Every Trip (Pt 1, 2, 3)

9:16 comedy series, ~60s per part. Setting: one group trip to Thailand (Bangkok, Krabi islands).
Format: big on-screen label per type, a short skit with the character's own voice, cutaways to
Sam staring into the camera like he's in a documentary he didn't sign up for. Every part ends on
the same WanderBuddy plug, then the approved brand outro.

## The 3 character models (recurring)

1. **SAM, the normal one.** 30, messy brown hair, light stubble, plain heather-grey tee, small
   backpack, permanently tired. Never speaks on camera. Communicates only through deadpan stares.
   He is all of us.
2. **BIANCA, the one in the red dress.** Late 20s, long dark waves, red satin dress, oversized
   sunglasses. 38 degrees and humid, not one drop of sweat. Has never carried her own bag.
3. **THE MILLERS, the family.** Greg (dad: bucket hat, sunburn, camera round his neck, too
   excited), Linda (mom: visor, tote bag, running on fumes), Tyler (12, iPad) and Maddie
   (9, arms permanently crossed).

## PART 1

**Cold open.** Bangkok airport gate. Sam sits holding a huge iced coffee, staring into the lens.
> SUPER: EVERY GROUP TRIP HAS THESE PEOPLE. PT 1

**1. THE TRIP CAPTAIN** (complete crash out)
Krabi pier. A ferry pulls away behind him. JAKE (lanyard, laminated itinerary, sweat patches)
jogs back to the group, smiling way too hard.
> JAKE: "Okay! Small update. The ferry left. The backup ferry also left. Nobody panic."
> (beat, the smile cracks)
> JAKE: "I'M PANICKING."
He flings the laminated itinerary into the sea.

**2. THE ONE IN THE RED DRESS** (and her simps)
Bangkok street market, slow motion. Bianca walks, flawless. Three guys trail her like
ducklings: one carries her bag, one holds a parasol over her, one fans her with a menu.
> BIANCA (not looking back): "I'm kinda thirsty."
All three sprint off in three different directions.
> SUPER: THE SIMPS (they came as a set)
Cut to Sam, drenched in sweat, carrying his own bag. Stare.

**3. THE COUPLE** (obsessed with each other, at war with each other)
Sunset beach bench.
> MIA: "You're literally the worst."
> LEO: "No, YOU'RE the worst."
They kiss.
> MIA: "I hate you."
> LEO: "I hate you more."
They kiss again. Pull back: Sam is sitting directly between them holding a coconut, dead inside.

**4. THE FAMILY** (and the kids who will remember none of it)
A stunning golden temple. Greg throws his arms wide.
> GREG: "Kids! Look! This temple is eight hundred years old!"
> TYLER (eyes on iPad): "Is the wifi eight hundred years old too?"
> LINDA: "This trip cost eleven thousand dollars."
> MADDIE: "Can we go back to the pool?"
> SUPER: THEY WILL REMEMBER NONE OF THIS.
> SUPER (2041): "wait, we went to Thailand?"

**5. THE ONE WHO'S BEEN HERE BEFORE**
Longtail boat. CHAD (linen shirt unbuttoned to the navel, sunglasses on his head).
> CHAD: "Oh, you're excited about THIS beach? Cute. I came in 2016, before it got touristy."
> CHAD (to the Thai boat driver): "Actually my guy, you're saying it wrong."
> DRIVER: "I'm from here."
> CHAD: "And yet."

**6. THE ONE WHO'S ALWAYS DRINKING**
Hotel breakfast buffet, 8:47 am. DANNY, sunglasses indoors, a bucket drink with four straws.
> LINDA: "Danny, it's breakfast."
> DANNY: "Exactly. Most important drink of the day."

**THE PLUG** (shared ending, all 3 parts). Sam, finally smiling, on a quiet beach with a small
group of friends he actually likes. He holds up his phone. Real WanderBuddy screens pop in.
> SAM (VO): "Or use WanderBuddy. Plan trips with people you actually want to travel with.
> Share your reels, get recommendations you actually want. And get reminders, so nobody
> loses their passport."
> CARDS: Plan with people you actually like. / Share reels, get recs you'll use. / Get reminders. Lose nothing.
> Brand outro.

## PART 2

**Cold open.** Hotel balcony. Sam, hair worse, eye bags worse.
> SUPER: PT 2. THEY MULTIPLIED.

**1. THE GROUP DAD** (27, not anyone's dad)
Hotel lobby. Bum bag, six passports on a lanyard, power bank, SPF 50, first aid kit, counting
heads with a finger.
> GROUP DAD: "Eleven, twelve, thirteen. Where's Danny?"
> FRIEND: "Bro, you're not my dad."
> GROUP DAD: "Then why do I have your passport, your charger, and your EpiPen?"

**2. THE WEATHER FREAK**
Blazing sunny beach, not a single cloud. She wears a full rain poncho and checks four weather
apps at once.
> WEATHER FREAK: "Thirty percent chance of rain at three fifteen."
> FRIEND: "It's literally sunny."
> WEATHER FREAK: "For now." (opens an umbrella)

**3. THE ONES WHO PACKED THEIR OWN FOOD**
Fancy hotel restaurant. The waiter hands over the menu. An Indian AUNTY reads the price,
smiles politely, and unzips a suitcase on the table: theplas, pickle jars, instant noodles,
a mini rice cooker.
> AUNTY: "Twenty-eight dollars? For noodles? Beta, I brought nine days of food."
Across the room an East Asian GRANDMA unzips her own suitcase: instant noodles, kimchi, a rice
cooker. The two women lock eyes and give one slow nod of deep respect.

**4. THE ONE WHO'S HERE FOR THE CULTURE**
BRAD: man bun, elephant pants, beaded bracelets, barefoot. He does a deep, slow wai to a
vending machine.
> BRAD: "Sawasdee krap. I'm not a tourist. I'm a traveller."
He bites into a toastie, eyes closed.
> BRAD: "Wow. The most authentic thing I've ever eaten."
Pull back: he is standing inside a 7-Eleven.

**5. THE LOCAL WHO HATES TOURISTS** (but has rent)
Tuk-tuk. The DRIVER beams at a group of tourists.
> DRIVER: "Welcome my friend! Best price! Very special for you!"
They climb in. He turns to the camera. The smile switches off.
> DRIVER (flat): "Every year. More of you."
He turns back, smile on full blast.
> DRIVER: "Seatbelt? No seatbelt!"

**6. THE ADRENALINE JUNKIE**
Cliff edge over turquoise water. JAX: shirtless, GoPro strapped to his forehead, energy drink.
> JAX: "How high is it?"
> GUIDE: "Too high."
> JAX: "SAY LESS." (sprints off the edge)
The guide sighs and looks at Sam. Sam looks at us.

**THE PLUG** (same as Part 1).

## PART 3

**Cold open.** Airport again. Sam alone, hugging his backpack like a life jacket.
> SUPER: PT 3. I SHOULD'VE STAYED HOME.

**1. THE ONE WHO LOSES EVERYTHING**
Hotel checkout. TOM, on his phone, pats every pocket.
> TOM (into the phone): "Guys, I can't find my phone."
> (everyone stares)
> TOM: "Also my wallet. And my passport. And where's my other shoe?"
He is wearing one flip-flop.

**2. THE FLASHY ONE**
Busy Bangkok street. RICKY: gold chains, logos on every inch, iced-out watch, filming himself.
> RICKY: "Yo chat, this place is SO safe. Look at the drip."
A scooter zips past. His chain is gone. Beat.
> RICKY: "It WAS safe."

**3. THE GUY AND HIS "GIRLFRIEND"**
Pool bar. RON (62, Hawaiian shirt, deep tan, gold chain) with CRYSTAL (24, glamorous, chewing
gum, bored).
> RON: "Everyone, this is my girlfriend. We met recently."
> CRYSTAL: "Hi! I'm..." (glances at Ron) "...what did we say my name was?"
> RON (quietly): "Crystal."
> CRYSTAL: "Crystal!" (checks her watch) "Babe, that's two hours. Same card?"

**4. THE CONTENT CREATOR**
Golden hour beach. ZOE with a tripod and ring light. The whole group stands waist-deep in the
sea, soaked and sunburnt.
> ZOE: "Okay, one more take. Walk into the ocean again, but candid."
> FRIEND: "That was take forty-seven."
> ZOE: "Candid-er."

**5. THE "I'LL SEND IT LATER" GUY**
Restaurant. The bill lands. KEVIN pats his pockets.
> KEVIN: "Can you get this one? I'll send it later."
> SUPER: DAY 1 ... DAY 4 ... DAY 9 (same clip, same line, three times)
> SUPER: HE DID NOT SEND IT LATER.

**6. FINALE**
Departures hall, the whole cast: Jake crying into the itinerary, Danny with a bucket at the
check-in desk, Tom in one flip-flop, the simps carrying Bianca's six suitcases, the kids on their
iPads. Sam in the middle. He stares into the lens for a very long time.
> SUPER: SAME TRIP. SAME PEOPLE. EVERY TIME.

**THE PLUG** (same as Part 1).
