# The Kinds of Travellers: script v5 (for review)

One group trip to Thailand, told in order across three parts, like a wildlife documentary.
Each part opens on an establishing shot with a title card and a narrator intro, so it is obvious
this is a trip. The narrator then sets the scene (where / when) before each character, and the
character's own skit lands the joke. About 60 seconds each.

On-screen text: the title card at the start of each part, plus captions on the narrator.
N = narrator. KEEP = existing clip, NEW = to generate.

---

## Part 1: Arrival

**Intro.** NEW establishing shot: a plane landing in Bangkok at golden hour, then the group
spilling out of arrivals into the heat with too many suitcases.
N: "Every year, millions of humans travel in groups. We followed one to Thailand."
TITLE: **The Kinds of Travellers** / Part 1: Arrival

1. **The group dad.** Hotel lobby, check-in, suitcases everywhere. NEW (khaki travel vest, a dozen pockets).
   N: "Every group has a dad."
   HIM (counting heads): "Where's Danny?" FRIEND: "Bro, you're not my dad."
   HIM (passport out of a vest pocket): "Then why do I have your passport?"
   N: "He is twenty-seven."

2. **The local.** Outside the hotel, a tuk-tuk. KEEP.
   N: "Outside, a local welcomes them."
   DRIVER (beaming): "Welcome my friend! Best price! Very special for you!"
   DRIVER (to camera, flat): "Every year. More of you."
   DRIVER (beaming again): "Seatbelt? No seatbelt!"

3. **The trek freak.** Same street, same tuk-tuk. KEEP.
   HIM: "Tuk-tuk? It's only nine kilometres. We walk!" FRIEND: "It's forty degrees."
   HIM: "Exactly. Free sauna!"
   N: "Homo cardio."

4. **The red dress.** The market. KEEP.
   N: "At the market, a rare sighting."
   HER: "I'm kinda thirsty." Her three simps sprint off in three directions.
   N: "They travel in packs."

5. **The activity-maxxer.** Next morning, dark hotel room, door bursts open. KEEP.
   N: "Day two. Five a.m."
   HER (headlamp, whistle): "Sunrise hike at five, temple at six, cooking class six-fifteen, elephants six-twenty!"
   FRIEND (under the duvet): "When do we sleep?" HER: "On the plane home!"
   N: "Activitas maximus."

6. **Danny.** Breakfast buffet. KEEP. (Pays off "Where's Danny?")
   N: "Breakfast. Danny has been found."
   MOM: "Danny. It's breakfast." DANNY (bucket drink): "Exactly. Most important drink of the day."
   N: "Homo alcoholicus."

7. **Plug.** NEW: a clean hotel lobby in chaos (whistle, arguing, someone panicking);
   Sam quietly stands up and walks out. Then the real app (Everyone gets a vote), then KEEP:
   Sam laughing on a longtail boat with friends he actually likes. Brand outro.
   N: "Or. Plan your next trip with people you actually like."
   Outro N: "WanderBuddy. Choose your herd wisely."

## Part 2: Into the Wild

**Intro.** NEW establishing shot: aerial over Krabi's islands, longtail boats below.
N: "Day four. The group ventures into the wild."
TITLE: **The Kinds of Travellers** / Part 2: Into the Wild

1. **The family.** A golden temple. KEEP.
   N: "Some travel with their young."
   DAD: "This temple is 800 years old!" SON (on iPad): "Is the wifi 800 years old too?"
   MOM: "This trip cost $11,000." GIRL: "Can we go back to the pool?"
   N: "The young will remember none of this."

2. **The culture guy.** KEEP.
   N: "Others come to find themselves."
   HIM: "Sawasdee krap. I'm not a tourist. I'm a traveller." (bites a toastie)
   "Wow. The most authentic thing I've ever eaten." (camera pulls back: a convenience store)

3. **The aunty.** Hotel restaurant. KEEP.
   N: "Dinner, at the hotel."

4. **The grandma.** Same restaurant. KEEP.
   N: "Across the room, another of her kind." (one slow, respectful nod)

5. **The weather freak.** Beach, perfect sky. KEEP.
   N: "Day five. Not a cloud for a thousand miles."
   HER (rain poncho): "Thirty percent chance of rain at three fifteen."
   FRIEND: "It's literally sunny." HER: "For now." (umbrella pops open)

6. **The couple.** Sunset bench, Sam squashed at the end. KEEP.

7. **The adrenaline junkie.** The cliff. KEEP.

8. **Plug.** KEEP: Sam in a hammock shares a reel, the real app (DM a reel, it's in the plan),

## Part 3: Going Home

**Intro.** NEW establishing shot: sunset over the sea, a plane crossing the sky; suitcases by a hotel door.
N: "The final days. The group must find its way home. Not all of them will make it."
TITLE: **The Kinds of Travellers** / Part 3: Going Home

1. **The FOMO guy.** The last beach day. KEEP.
   N: "The last beach day."
   HIM (scrolling): "Guys. What if the beach next door is better?" FRIEND: "It's the same beach."
   HIM (squinting down the shore): "Is it, though?"
   N: "Homo fomo."

2. **The content creator.** Same beach, golden hour. KEEP.
   N: "Some must document everything."
   HER: "Okay, one more take! Walk into the ocean again, but candid."
   FRIEND (soaked): "That was take forty-seven!" HER: "Candid-er."

3. **Ron and Crystal.** The pool bar. NEW.
   N: "At the pool bar, an unusual pairing."
   RON: "Everyone, this is my girlfriend. We met... recently."
   HER: "Hi! I'm... what did we say my name was?" RON (whisper): "Crystal." HER: "Crystal!"
   HER (checks watch): "Babe, it's been two hours. That's extra."
   N: "Nature has many partnerships."

4. **The flashy guy.** The last night out in the city. NEW.
   N: "The last night in the city."
   HIM (filming himself, gold chains): "Twenty-four karat. Feel the weight."
   A scooter zips past; the chain is gone. He touches his bare neck.
   N: "He can no longer feel the weight."

5. **Kevin.** Three dinners. KEEP.
   N: "Every dinner, the same ritual. Day one."
   KEVIN: "Can you get this one? I'll send it later."
   N: "Day four." KEVIN (beach bar): same line. N: "Day nine." KEVIN (airport cafe): same line.
   N: "He will not send it later."

6. **The forgetful one.** Checkout. NEW.
   N: "Checkout."
   HIM (patting every pocket, phone in hand): "Has anyone seen my phone?" FRIEND: "It's in your hand."
   HIM: "Okay. Has anyone seen my passport?" FRIEND: "Where's your other shoe?"
   (he looks down at one bare foot)
   N: "It will never be found."

7. **Departures.** The whole cast in chaos, Sam in the middle. KEEP.
   N: "Same trip. Same people. Every time."

8. **Plug.** KEEP: Sam's phone buzzes in his hotel room, he takes his passport from the drawer,
   the real app (Passport panic, cancelled). Brand outro.
   N: "Or. Let WanderBuddy remind you before you leave."
   Outro N: "WanderBuddy. Leave the rest of the herd at home."
