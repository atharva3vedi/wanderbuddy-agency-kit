# The People You Meet On Every Trip: script v4 (for review)

Three videos, about 60 seconds each. Format: a deadpan British narrator gives each "species"
a short setup or a button (often a mock-Latin name), never explains what you can already see,
and never freezes the picture to talk. Captions follow the narrator.

Legend: KEEP = existing clip, NEW = re-shoot. N = narrator.
