# WanderBuddy — bespoke product-introduction video (NOTES / brief)

Built with the VidFarm `unique-product-explainers` method (fetched from
vidfarm.cc/experimental/unique-product-explainers.md), stacked with the
per-video `product-explainer` shape. Cost mode: **minimize ($0)** — local
Kokoro TTS, local headless-Chrome frame render, ffmpeg, procedural CC0-style
music bed. No cloud, no wallet.

## What the product is (recon done from the source repo, not scraped)
WanderBuddy = one app to plan and run a **group trip**: group chat, an AI-built
day-by-day itinerary, split-cost budget, a live map of the whole crew, members,
documents. Brand identity (lib/theme/app_colors.dart): **"Mission Control"** —
electric azure on an ink-navy canvas, signal-amber accent, teal. Dark-forward
brand → the video is built **dark**.

## PLAIN-ENGLISH LINE (must be understood by t=5s) — written verbatim
> "WanderBuddy plans your whole trip in one app."
Spoken as VO line 2 (t≈3.7s) AND shown as hero type in scene 2. Not only in captions.

## Concept (derived from the brand's own material)
**The crew converging on the map.** The app's own live-map line is "Everyone's
converging on Wat Arun — today's meet point." Four member pins (You/Maya/Leo/
Priya) start scattered across a dark Bangkok map and, by the payoff, snap
together onto one glowing meet-point pin: *one app that keeps the group together*.
Avoids the DIFFERENTIATION forbidden shapes (no progress rail, no concentric
rings resolving, no "map pins dropping" — this is characters *converging*).

## Assigned direction
- Format 1080x1920 (9:16). Theme DARK (ink-navy #0B141F). Energy: bright, brisk, punchy.
- Type: Montserrat 800/900 (allowed display face), kinetic per-word captions = hero type.
- VO: **bf_emma** (Kokoro local) — bright & brisk; justified: sold on speed/togetherness.
- Audio: VO + light procedural music bed + kinetic captions. User adds a trending track at post.

## Golden-rule compliance
- Motion from frame 1 (map drift + avatars drifting + pin pulse at t=0).
- Frame 0 = complete headline "GROUP TRIPS, MINUS THE CHAOS" + plain-line pill + wordmark = 3 runs, works as thumbnail.
- Relevant sticker in first beat = the map pin (subject of the story).
- Product plainly named by t=5s in VO + on-screen type.
- One sticker on screen at a time; CTA settled >= 2s before end.

## Accuracy
Claims only what the app shows: group chat, AI itinerary, split costs (฿ demo
data), live map, members. No store/price/rating claims. CTA = wanderbuddyapp.com.
Names in screenshots (Maya/Leo/Priya) are the app's own demo/seed data.
