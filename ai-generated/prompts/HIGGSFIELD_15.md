# 15 Paste-Ready Concepts — WanderBuddy

Each block is one video. Paste a single block into a Claude session running the
Higgsfield MCP. Read `marketing/higgsfield_skills.md` first: it holds the cast
references, the house look, the negative prompt, the motion vocabulary and the
outro spec, so none of that is repeated here.

**Key:** `IMG` = image prompt · `MOT` = motion preset · `VO` = spoken line,
hyphenated for pronunciation · `TXT` = on-screen text ·
`SCREEN` = real app recording from `marketing/app_capture/`, never generated.

Every video: 9:16 · 5s per shot · outro from the skills file ·
CTA `Android · Google Play`.

---

## 01 — THE JOB
**Feature:** everything. **Outro:** siren variant.
**HOOK** Six people in a warehouse planning something. **PROBLEM** Coordinating a group is an operation. **SOLUTION** Every feature, deployed with total seriousness. **CTA** For groups with a plan.

Shot like a Michael Mann film that ran out of money. **Nobody winks. No comedy music.** The moment anyone smirks, it dies.

1. `IMG` six figures around a table in a stripped warehouse, single caged work-lamp overhead, blueprints, cold blue light, faces half in shadow, cigarette smoke
   `MOT` overhead descend
   `VO` (Nneka, quiet) "Ev-ery-one sees the same plan. In real time. No ex-cep-tions."
2. `SCREEN` shared itinerary being edited live, day 3 visible
   `VO` (Nneka) "Day three. Four-teen hun-dred."
   `TXT` the entry reads `14:00 — THE VAULT`
3. `IMG` MAYA in a doorway, rain behind her, looking straight down the lens, deciding
   `MOT` slow push in
   `SCREEN` cut to a join request being approved
   `VO` (Tobi, flat) "You ap-prove ev-ery-one who gets in."
4. `IMG` DIEGO and PLOY in a van, headsets on, talking over each other urgently
   `MOT` snorricam
   `VO` Portuguese and Thai simultaneously, no English
   `TXT` two caption streams, both flowing
   `VO` (overlay) "The safe-crack-er does-n't speak Eng-lish. No-bo-dy's ask-ing him to."
5. `SCREEN` visa checker: passport in, destination in, result, official link
   `VO` (Tobi, dead flat) "An-y pass-port. An-y des-ti-na-tion. Check the vi-sa be-fore you fly."
   **Hold one beat too long. This is the punchline. Do not sell it.**
6. `IMG` the same six walking away down a wet street at dawn, backs to camera, carrying nothing suspicious
   `MOT` slow pull back
   `TXT` `FOR GROUPS WITH A PLAN`

*It never says a crime happened. The audience builds the heist out of real features, so the joke is theirs and you made no claim. That is also why it clears ad review.*

## 02 — THE PANEL
**Feature:** everything, described badly. **Outro:** standard.
**HOOK** Five beautiful people being insufferable. **PROBLEM** Nobody can explain why an app is good. **SOLUTION** Someone who wasn't asked. **CTA** What she said.

Panel shots lit like a fragrance campaign. Payoff shot lit like a phone flash in a kitchen. That contrast is the gag.

1. `IMG` MAYA in a wine bar, immaculate, backlit amber, mid-gesture like she is presenting a thesis
   `MOT` slow push in
   `VO` "It's a syn-chro-nous mul-ti-par-ti-ci-pant it-in-er-ar-y ar-chi-tec-ture."
   `TXT` small, corner: `SHARED ITINERARY`
2. `IMG` TOBI against concrete, unreasonably good lighting for a man in a windbreaker
   `MOT` whip pan
   `VO` "Fun-da-men-tal-ly, an in-ter-op-er-a-bil-i-ty lay-er for ir-rec-on-cil-a-ble tem-per-a-ments."
   `TXT` `LIVE CONVERSATION`
3. `IMG` NNEKA, gold hoops catching a rim light, arms folded, absolutely certain
   `MOT` crash zoom in
   `VO` "I would char-ac-ter-ise it as com-pu-ta-tion-al fis-cal rec-on-cil-i-a-tion."
   `TXT` `EXPENSE SPLITTING`
4. `IMG` SAM, same beautiful lighting, visibly out of his depth, sweating
   `MOT` static handheld drift
   `VO` "It's... pol-y-cen-tric."
   `TXT` `HE DOESN'T KNOW WHAT THAT MEANS`
   Hold one beat past comfortable.
5. **Hard cut. Kill the lighting.** `IMG` PLOY at a cluttered hostel counter eating instant noodles from the pot, direct flash, not looking at the lens
   `MOT` handheld follow, slightly crooked
   `VO` (flat, mouth full) "We all use the same one. No-bo-dy's trans-lat-ing. No-bo-dy owes an-y-bo-dy. It's good."
   She goes back to the noodles.
6. `TXT` `WHAT SHE SAID`

*Zero-credit variants: swap the payoff to the night porter, the nine-year-old at the next table, the bartender, or someone's mother on a video call nobody ended. Four videos, one production run.*

## 03 — NOBODY SWITCHED TO ENGLISH
**Feature:** Live Conversation. **Outro:** standard. **This is the one nobody can copy. Spend here.**
**HOOK** 1.5 seconds of untranslated Thai. **PROBLEM** Someone always gets demoted to the quiet one. **SOLUTION** Everyone speaks their own language. **CTA** Google Play.

1. `IMG` PLOY at a Bangkok night market, talking fast and animated straight to camera, harsh flash, steam and neon behind her
   `MOT` handheld follow
   `VO` Thai, fast. **No subtitles for the first 1.5 seconds.** Let it be uncomfortable.
   `TXT` Thai caption appears late, English underneath
2. `IMG` TOBI opposite her, glasses fogged from food steam, answering flatly
   `MOT` whip pan
   `VO` German
   `TXT` German caption, English underneath
3. `IMG` two-shot, DIEGO and NNEKA both talking at once, gesturing over each other
   `MOT` crash zoom out
   `VO` Portuguese and English colliding
   `TXT` two caption streams stacked, both flowing, neither dropping
   **Hold this shot. Every other translation app dies here.**
4. `SCREEN` Live Conversation, speaker names colour-tagged, lines arriving in the viewer's language
   `VO` (dry) "Ev-ery-one's phone hears on-ly its own-er. That's why it does-n't fall a-part when you talk o-ver each oth-er."
5. `IMG` NNEKA leaning back laughing, phone flat on the table beside her
   `MOT` static handheld drift
   `VO` "I have-n't spo-ken Eng-lish in four days."
6. `TXT` `NOBODY GETS DEMOTED TO THE QUIET ONE`

## 07 — SIX MISSED CALLS
**Feature:** safety kit, visa. **Outro:** siren variant. **Not funny. Do not make it funny.**
**HOOK** A hand patting an empty pocket. **PROBLEM** It goes wrong at the worst possible hour. **SOLUTION** The number, the embassy, your coordinates, the checklist. **CTA** Built after a night like this.

1. `IMG` POV looking down at an empty jacket pocket, hand patting it, crowded night street blurred beyond
   `MOT` handheld follow
   `VO` street noise, suddenly too loud
2. `IMG` PLOY under green-white fluorescent in a bare police station corridor, plastic chairs, exhausted
   `MOT` static handheld drift
   `VO` room tone only. No narration. Let it sit.
3. `SCREEN` safety screen: local emergency number, embassy finder, exact coordinates, lost-phone checklist
   `VO` (quiet) "The lo-cal e-mer-gen-cy num-ber, wher-ev-er you are. Your em-bas-sy. Your ex-act co-or-di-nates, read-y to send."
4. `IMG` MAYA pushing through the station door, coat over pyjamas, phone held out screen-first
   `MOT` handheld follow
   `VO` door, footsteps
5. `SCREEN` visa screen: passport and destination, status, link to the official page
   `VO` "And the vi-sa an-swer from the act-u-al im-mi-gra-tion page."
6. `IMG` wide, all six outside the station at first light, someone handing Ploy a coffee, nobody talking
   `MOT` slow pull back
   `VO` (Ploy) "I met these peo-ple three weeks a-go."
   `TXT` `BUILT AFTER A NIGHT LIKE THIS`

*Claim discipline: the app gives you the number, the embassy and your coordinates. It does not contact emergency services for you. Say it exactly that way.*

## 11 — TERMS AND CONDITIONS
**Feature:** pricing transparency, no ads, no trackers. **Outro:** standard.
**HOOK** Someone reading a statement to camera under a bare bulb. **PROBLEM** Every app hides the arithmetic. **SOLUTION** The app shows you its own invoices. **CTA** Google Play.

Play it exactly like a proof-of-life video. Deadpan, flat, uncomfortable.

1. `IMG` NNEKA under a single bare bulb, holding a sheet of paper, reading directly to camera, blank wall behind
   `MOT` slow push in
   `VO` "I am read-ing this of my own free will."
2. `VO` (continuing, same shot) "The store takes fif-teen per-cent. The A-P-I bill is print-ed in the app. What is left o-ver goes to one de-vel-op-er."
   `TXT` numbers slam on screen as she says them
3. `SCREEN` the transparency screen, real numbers, scrolling slowly
   `VO` "You can read it your-self. It's in the set-tings."
4. `IMG` TOBI just off frame, holding a card with `NO ADS` written on it in marker
   `MOT` whip pan
   `VO` "There are no ads. There are no track-ers. The free app is the whole app."
5. `IMG` NNEKA lowering the paper, looking straight down the lens, saying nothing
   `MOT` static handheld drift
   `TXT` `WE PUBLISH OUR OWN INVOICES`

*This is the concept most likely to get you written about. It is also the cheapest: five stills, four clips, no crowd, no action.*
