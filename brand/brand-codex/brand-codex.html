01What we are

WanderBuddy is a travel marketplace. Agencies and trip captains list their departures for free, travellers find them on a map and buy a seat, and then the whole group lives the trip together in the app. We take a fee on each seat booked.

Today the agency desk pays the bills: the CRM and the WhatsApp assistant that agencies run their departures on. Every departure it runs brings a group of travellers into the app, and that is how the marketplace fills.

Name
WanderBuddy. One word, capital W and capital B.Never "Wander Buddy", "wanderbuddy" or "WB" in copy. For voice models write "Wander-Buddy", so it is never heard as "Wonder Buddy".
Tagline
Travel together, perfectly in sync.The sign-off. Use it whole.
For travellers
Find a trip, hold your seat, and travel it together.Until bookings are live in the app: "Plan your trip together", with votes, reels and costs in one place.
For agencies
List your trips on WanderBuddy, free.The hook in every agency reel and DM. The desk comes after: "and if you want it, a CRM".
Where
Southeast Asia first: Thailand, Bali, Vietnam, Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines.Prices in USD. Examples, numbers and places from the region. No India references.

03How it feels

01

The host is the brand.

On anything a traveller sees about a trip, the agency or captain comes first: name, logo, verified tick. WanderBuddy is the credit line, where the seat is held and where the trip is lived.

02

Only what is live.

Every claim is checked against what ships. Pilot work is "built with you", never shown as live. No "book on WanderBuddy" until payments are on in that country. The not-shipped list lives in website/src/lib/site.ts.

03

Real, never generated-looking.

Real app screens, real recordings, real photos from past departures. No gradient hero over three cards, no buzzwords, no synthesized chimes, no stock or generated images shown as a trip.

04

One thing at a time.

One headline per frame, with no kicker above it and no grey line under it. One control per action. If a detail matters, it goes in the main text, in full ink.

05

Calm, and every touch lands.

Solid grounds, no glow. Each press has weight, each new trip gets its moment, and then the screen settles. Excitement comes from the trip, not from the interface.

07Pictures and motion

The real product is the picture. We show our own screens, real places and real departures, and point at the thing that matters with one azure ring.

Screens
Real app and CRM screens, never mock-ups dressed up as the product.Store frames and carousel cards: one headline, one real screen, nothing else.
The ring
One azure ring around the card, button or message we are talking about. One at a time.It is the only flourish. It replaces the old ripple and bullet-time freeze.
Reels
Animated from real screens, with captions burned in: white WbSans on a dark pill. A 0.3 second crossfade into the end card.The demo agency is always Lantern Trips. Agency screens shown: Inbox and Home.
End card
White peaks rise from the sea, the wave forms, the wordmark lifts, on brand blue.
Photos
Real places from the region. On a listing, the host's photos from past departures, or none.No stock or generated image is ever shown as a trip someone can buy.
In the app
Every press gives a little weight back. Big moments (a trip created, everyone ready) get one short beat, then the screen settles.Reduced motion is honoured everywhere.
Made-up names
Lantern Trips and Kite Trips are ours to use. Never a real agency's name or trip in an example.

08Talking to travellers

We write like the friend who has already been and would never let you get stranded. Specific over scenic. A verb doing real work. One idea per line.

We say
  • "Vote on dates. The plan follows the group."
  • "Send a reel to the trip on WhatsApp. It comes back as places on your map."
  • "Going alone? Start with Just me."
  • "Pickup is at 7:30, from the hotel lobby."
  • Lead with planning together, not with AI.
We never say
  • "Revolutionary all-in-one travel ecosystem."
  • "AI plans your whole trip in seconds."
  • "Meet other solo travellers."
  • "Book on WanderBuddy" before payments are live there.
  • No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no rupee prices.
Words
Trip. Departure, for an agency's dated trip. Host, for the agency or captain. Travellers, never "users" or "pax". Hold my spot. The assistant, never "the bot".

09Talking to agencies

With agencies we sound like an operator who has run a departure, not like software. Their day is WhatsApp at 11 pm and a Canva itinerary at midnight. Open with the free listing, name their day, and put their brand in front of ours.

Open with
"List your trips on WanderBuddy, free."Then the desk, as an option: "and if you want it, a CRM, so enquiries stop getting lost in chats".
Their day first
The job they do today, in their words from our calls: pickup questions, passport copies, the PDF nobody opens.
Their brand on top
Travellers see the agency's name, logo and colours on the join page, the itinerary and the WhatsApp assistant. WanderBuddy is the credit line.
They set it up
Each connection (Google Drive, WhatsApp, Instagram) is linked by the agency alone, with short numbered steps.One shared sign-in link for every agency; it opens their own workspace.
Numbers
USD, with what the setup buys: the import, their brand, their itinerary design, the WhatsApp hookup, training.Prices belong in calls and the deck, never in a reel. Hours saved come from a real departure; until then they are targets.
A person sends it
Founder to founder, from the founder's own Instagram and WhatsApp, or from Vaibhav by email. First name, their trips, one ask.No auto-DMs, no bulk tools, no cold messages from the WanderBuddy WhatsApp number.
We say
  • "Your trips can sit in front of travellers who are already planning one."
  • "Your travellers ask the assistant, not you. It answers from your trip plan, day and night."
  • "Give us your next departure."
  • One job per sentence. Their words, our fix.
We never say
  • "Revolutionise your agency with AI."
  • "Get 3x more bookings." No promised results or cost per booking.
  • "Join 200+ agencies." No scale we do not have.
  • No feature grids, no "seamless", no "end-to-end platform".

A listing sells one departure for one host. Write it so a traveller can decide in thirty seconds on a phone.

Hold my spot

Free. Held for 7 days while Kite Trips confirms.

Why it works

  1. Host and tick before the title. The traveller knows who they are buying from.
  2. Dates, price and seats left at the first glance, in USD.
  3. A time or a place in every day line. Nothing a brochure could say about anywhere.
  4. What is not included, said as plainly as what is.
  5. The button says what it costs, nothing, and how long the hold lasts.
$890Agency trip Traveller trip
Honest numbers
Seats left is the real count. No countdown timers, no fake "Full", no review counts we do not have.
Who it suits
Pace, group size, rooms. Solo travellers book one seat like anyone else: "Going alone? Book one seat."
Money words
"Hold my spot" is free and says how long it holds. "Pay deposit" appears only where deposits are live, and says the money goes to the host. The cancellation policy is the host's, in their words.
After the hold
The next message comes from the host. Once booked, the listing becomes the trip: same name, same dates, now with the group in it.
We say
  • "Six days in Northern Thailand with Kite Trips. 14 to 19 Jan. From $890. 4 of 12 seats left."
  • "Hold my spot. Free, held for 7 days while the host confirms."
  • Every line answers a question a traveller would otherwise DM the host about.
We never say
  • "Once in a lifetime! Only 2 spots left!!"
  • "Best price guaranteed" or "Fully refundable", unless the host's policy says so.
  • "Loved by 1,000+ travellers" without the data behind it.

Colourway

Space
A full peak-height of air on every side. 24px minimum on screen.
Never
A sun, a third colour, a gradient, an outline, recoloured peaks or a stretched tile.

12The founder channel

The founder is a brand surface. When Atharva posts, it should sound like the same person who wrote this codex: sharp, generous, specific. Not a company account wearing a face.

Build in public
Show the work: what shipped, the numbers, the near-misses.
Take positions
Have opinions about travel and about how agencies work. A post any app founder could write is not worth posting.
Pay people back
Treat early travellers and founding agencies like insiders: name them, thank them, reward them.
Honest receipts
Real footage, real features, real numbers. Credibility is the whole account.
